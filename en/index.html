About us

Established in 2011, CAM NINH COMPANY LIMITED has made great strides in supplying industrial equipment solutions, installation, testing and commissioning of specialised industrial systems in Oil & Gas, security surveillance, fire alarm equipment, telecom, and control and visual monitoring systems. We want to bring our customers the best services, products and applications in every field we work in, so we keep looking for and building solutions that are technically optimal, reasonable in cost and easy to operate, and above all suited to today's operating conditions in Vietnam and across Southeast Asia.

With us, you will experience the best, most complete services at the most competitive prices. You can rely on our service with confidence.

CAM NINH COMPANY LIMITED knows that only outstanding product and service quality can prove lasting value. That is why we will always bring our customers the best services and the fullest value.
